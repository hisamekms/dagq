# Plan review by hand, and an unresponsive planner

Read this when the attention `plan review by hand` or `check the planner` reaches the person (the `dagq-recover` skill, section 8). Act only on the person's word; `ready --bypass-review` follows the rules of section 8.

Attention `plan review by hand` (`kind` `plan_review_failed`): the headless plan review of a proposal could not start, timed out or printed no valid verdict; the proposal stays `submitted` and held, and is not reviewed again by itself. Read `last_error` and the job's files under `<queue dir>/plan-reviews/<plan review id>/` (the job's ID, not the proposal's), `"$DAGQ" proposal show ID` and the tasks, and bring the choice to the person: `ready --bypass-review` each task, send it through plan review again as it is (`"$DAGQ" submit --proposal ID`, typed by the person in their own terminal without `DAGQ_ROLE`; people no longer open planners), or `cancel` the tasks. A change to the plan goes as a planning request that names the proposal's tasks (`request add --ref task:N`, `skills/dagq-inbox/reference/requests.md`). `check the planner` (`planner_unresponsive`): the person looks at that planner (`dagq planners`, `"$DAGQ" planner screen ID`; a headless planner has no screen and the reply names its `turns/`); nothing is closed or readied for it. Without a proposal (`status` `working`, `last_error` naming the planner) it is a runtime planner nothing was seen of: the person looks at it the same way and, for an interactive one that is stuck, sends it `/exit` (`"$DAGQ" planner send ID --key exit`), or leaves it; the supervisor closes runtime planners (there is no `planner close`).
